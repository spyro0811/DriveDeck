# Plaid Track preview

Choose **Plaid Track**, immediately after Model S in the deck library. Its **Display** button opens a visual picker for:

- **Temp**: an underside thermal schematic, coolant/oil readings, tire pressures, estimated G force and speed.
- **Clean**: fully monochrome instruments and a detailed front-facing silver drivetrain render, with front/rear brake and motor bars and engine temperature; portrait displays use a stacked arrangement.
- **We’ve gone plaid!**: the current BeamNG vehicle image with a full-gauge Plaid tunnel of glowing rays and expanding rectangular portals.
- **Plaid Sensors**: the exposed thermal chassis over the Plaid road grid, with sensor readings.

The two Plaid launch views show the preparation scene until the vehicle first moves. A new vehicle or a disconnected session resets preparation. Reinstall the bundled companion and restart BeamNG to receive the new brake surface temperatures. Motor temperature remains unavailable when the game does not expose it. Temperatures and pressures use telemetry; missing readings remain blank. The underbody is a schematic, not a scan of each vehicle. G force is an estimate from speed and reported map heading, and needs fresh companion position data.

Civetta’s display selector is lower and the hidden fullscreen menu has a smaller reveal area, so reaching for the selector does not open that menu.

This and future builds are BeamNG-only, per the user’s request. The earlier experimental game integration is archived outside the packaged source.

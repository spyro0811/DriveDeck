BEHIND THE COVER

Your album. In motion.

DriveDeck reads the song title, artist, and album from your Windows media player. When you enable animated artwork, it uses that metadata to find a matching album in Apple’s public catalog, then checks the album’s Apple Music page for animated cover artwork.

  1. Match the albumDifferent editions can have different artwork. DriveDeck checks matching streaming editions too.
  2. Play the cover animationWhen Apple publishes a compatible animation, it loops behind your music. You can still listen through Spotify or another player.
  3. Keep a cover readyIf animation is missing or fails, the regular album cover stays visible. Source order and animation can be changed in the desktop app.

Connect BeamNG.drive

  1. Open the game’s Options.Find Protocols under Other or Advanced, depending on your game version.
  2. Enable OutGauge UDP.Set the address to 127.0.0.1 and the port to the number below. Set the update rate to 30–60 Hz.
  3. Spawn or reload your vehicle.Press Ctrl + R. Your instruments connect when packets arrive.

Another dashboard may already use 4444. Choose a free port and match it in the game.
